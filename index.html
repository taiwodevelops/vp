About

Who I Am

A passionate leader, dedicated student, and advocate for change.

Leadership is not about being in charge. It's about taking care of those in your charge.

I'm Alex Rivera, a third-year Political Science student with a minor in Public Policy. Over the past two years, I've served as the Class Representative, led the Student Advisory Council, and founded the Campus Mentorship Program.

My mission is to create a faculty where every student — regardless of background, major, or year — feels seen, heard, and empowered to succeed.

FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Everything you need to know about my campaign and vision.

I'm running because I believe our faculty can do more — for students, by students. I've seen firsthand the challenges we face, and I know we have the talent and passion to overcome them. I want to build a faculty where every student thrives.

My top priority is student mental wellness and academic support. I will work to expand counseling services, create peer support networks, and ensure that every student has access to the resources they need to succeed both academically and personally.

I will hold weekly open office hours, host monthly town halls, and create a digital feedback platform where students can share ideas and concerns. Every voice matters, and I will make sure yours is heard at every level of decision-making.

I've served as Class Representative, led the Student Advisory Council, and founded the Campus Mentorship Program. These experiences have given me a deep understanding of student needs and the skills to drive meaningful change at the faculty level.

You can support by voting on election day, sharing my vision with your friends, volunteering with the campaign, or reaching out to me directly. Every voice and every action makes a difference. Let's build this future together.

If elected, I will hit the ground running. My first 100 days will focus on listening to students, building coalitions, and launching our key initiatives. I will keep you updated every step of the way through regular newsletters and open forums.
